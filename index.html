Gettysburg National Military Park

Gettysburg National Military Park is the perfect place for a Civil War History lover. It contains multiple monuments in rememberance if the Gettysburg battle.

Hershey Park

Hershey offers the perfect family vacation day. It provides a variety of activities such as roller coasters, waterparks, Zoo America, as wells as restaurants

State Capitol

Pennsylvania state capitol is a beautiful architectural monument surrounded by fountains and parks. Inside tours are offered at location.

Pine Grove Furnace State Park

Pine Grove Furnaace Park is located south central Pennsylvania. It has two main lakes provide different spots to spend a relaxing family day.

Lancaster Cental Market

Lancaster Central Market was established in 1730. It is very active with a variety of sales of fruits, vegetables, orders and souveniers.
