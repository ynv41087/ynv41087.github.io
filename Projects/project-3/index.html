San Francisco Coffee Seasoned with Love

Our San Francisco inspired coffee contains the coffee Aroma, and the beautiful familiar coffeehouse representations that lead to our love. Each cup is made with love and blended carefully to provide customers the closest flavor to San Francisco’s coffee cups.

Each coffee bean was picked from the finest locally sorted beans, providing a variety of flavors and roast types.

Season Signatures

Our Menu favorites and Seasonal Signatures mostly include warm coffee cappuccinos and pumpkin spice flavors. Although coffee is our main drink, other drinks and accompanying appetizers are served such as freshly baked muffins, bagels and croissants.

Be Part of the Community

Brews & Bonds provides a variety of family and group events. We believe that the love that brought us together may be enjoyed by everyone in Harrisburg. Our main events include live acoustic music, coffee tasting and book club.

Each day of the week is a fun day to taste and share with others. Schedule is provided in Events.
